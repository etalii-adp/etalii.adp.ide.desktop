# PROV-O — the W3C provenance ontology

**What it demonstrates.** A mid-size real standard read as a schema: 39 declared classes with
their asserted hierarchy, the object and datatype properties between them with domains and
ranges, 8 union expressions, 4 disjointness axioms, and one restriction — the shape of an
ontology a reader is likely to meet in the wild, small enough to take in at one zoom level.

As drawn: 42 elements, 83 axiom edges. It opens with no validation findings.
