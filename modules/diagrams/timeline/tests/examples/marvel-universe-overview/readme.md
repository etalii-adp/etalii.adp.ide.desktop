# The Marvel universe at a glance

A timeline of 47 key events of the Marvel Cinematic Universe's story, from Schmidt taking the Tesseract to the Fantastic Four's ship arriving, in story order, with 52 connections. Open `marvel-universe-overview.adp`; nothing needs configuring first. The full story, 428 events, is in [`../marvel-universe/`](../marvel-universe/).

## What it shows

Only what happens inside the story world: the origins, the Infinity Stones, the Snap and its undoing, and the Multiverse. No release dates, castings or studio news.

- **Each box is as long as its label needs.** The label's width was measured at the canvas's 12-unit text in the widest of the fonts a host may draw it with, plus room for the box's ends.
- **Time runs left to right, but the dates mean nothing.** The ruler's years are only a scale.
- **No two connections cross.** The places were found by a search that first keeps lines from crossing, then puts an event with a single connection right beside the event it connects to: before it when that event comes later, after it when it came earlier. All 13 such events sit there.
- **As many connections as possible run left to right**, from the right end of one box to the left end of the next: 43 of the 52. A chain longer than the timeline is wide starts again further down, and those 9 connections loop back.
- **Every event has at least one connection, and rows only stack boxes.**
- **A connection leads from an event to one that follows from it.** Here it skips the full story's steps in between, to the nearest key event.

## How it was made

The key events were picked from the full story in [`../marvel-universe/`](../marvel-universe/), and their connections follow its links. The labels are written in our own words; no text, image or logo is taken from any source. The order and links were written from the researchers' knowledge and were not checked one by one against a source.
